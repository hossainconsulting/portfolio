# Marketing: the YouTube playbook, sized for one person and no ad budget

Source: Google Australia, *The YouTube Era of Creator Marketing: A Playbook
for Success* (2026, six pages). It is sales collateral for Google's own ad
products, and every statistic in it is Google-commissioned, so the numbers
are directional. The framework underneath is still the right one for us.

## What the playbook says

| Their framework | Their claim | Their evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Bring** best-performing assets to YouTube | Content built for persistent discovery, not viral moments; 40% of views and 30% of clicks on sponsored videos happen more than 30 days after publish | Agentio 2026; Google internal Demand Gen results (+40% conversion value) |
| **Build** creator partnerships | 77% of Australians say YouTube has the most trusted creator content; campaigns with creator assets perform 20% better | Google/Kantar Future of Video AU 2025-26; Google internal Jan 2025-Jan 2026 |
| **Boost** creator content with paid media | Video Reach for awareness, Video View for consideration, Demand Gen for leads and sales; YouTube ROI 15% above paid social | Analytic Edge MMM meta-analysis 2021-2025 (3.36 vs 2.91); Canva (-8% CPA, +9% conversions); eBay (2.6x interaction rate) |

Underlying claim worth keeping: 75% of Australians name YouTube as the video
platform they watch most, and it is the place people go to research, vet and
decide on a product or service. That is exactly the moment a trades business
owner decides whether to trust an AI agent or a Salesforce consultant.

## What it means for us

We are not a brand hiring creators. We are the creator. The recorded demos
already planned in home-services-ai, the engagement write-ups in the
portfolio, and the honest trading record are the assets. Applied:

**Bring.** Every deliverable that already exists becomes a video before it
becomes anything else:
- The 60-second agent demos (Quote Triage, Notes to Invoice, After-Hours
  Triage) go to YouTube as Shorts and as one long "three tools in five
  minutes" video, then get embedded on the platform's offering cards.
- One 8-12 minute walkthrough per Salesforce engagement (Sunrise Solar,
  Meridian, TradeLink): the problem, the data model, the decision log, the
  result. Long-form is where the 30-days-later views come from.
- The trading lab's crash and base-rate reports become a monthly "what the
  data says" video once real data is collected. The honesty *is* the content.

**Build.** Partnerships, at our scale, mean two things:
- Guest on trades-industry and small-business YouTube channels in Australia
  (plumbing, electrical, solar) with a demo, not a pitch. The After-Hours
  Triage gas-leak escalation is the sixty seconds that earns trust.
- Later, when there are paying customers, a customer-led video: their
  enquiry inbox before and after. That is the creator asset the playbook is
  describing, made with a customer instead of an influencer.

**Boost.** Not until organic proves something. Then a Demand Gen campaign
with a hard weekly cap, aimed at the one video whose organic watch-time and
click-through are best, pointing at a single offering page. Measure cost per
sign-up on the platform's admin page against the subscription price; stop
the moment it exceeds one month of revenue per customer.

## Sequence and budget

| Stage | Trigger | Spend |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Channel and first six videos (three demos, three walkthroughs) | platform deployed | $0, a microphone |
| 2. Two guest appearances | first ten subscribers | $0 |
| 3. Customer story video | first customer willing to be filmed | $0 |
| 4. Demand Gen test on the best video | 1,000 organic views on any video | $10/day for 30 days, then decide |

## How we will know it is working

The platform's admin page already counts users and MRR. Add a `?src=` query
on every YouTube link so sign-ups carry their source into D1, and report
sign-ups by source in the Monday digest. If YouTube is not the top source
within three months of stage 1, the videos are the problem, not the channel.
