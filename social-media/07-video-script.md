# 7. Short-form video script: why the after-hours agent refuses to book a gas leak

**Length:** 45 seconds
**Platform:** Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, re-cut from the LinkedIn native version
**Topic:** an AI booking agent that recognises an emergency and hands off to a human instead of booking a slot
**Audience:** owners of trades and home-services businesses considering an AI answering or booking tool
**Desired action:** follow, and visit the portfolio to see the transcripts
**Source:** Home Services AI, project 4, After-Hours Agent. The escalation cases live in the committed transcripts.
**Recording:** phone, front camera, natural light, sitting at the desk with the laptop visible. Captions burned in. No music.

## Notes for recording

- Say the times out loud in the first take and cut them later. It keeps the pace.
- The pattern interrupt at 17s must be a real cut, not a zoom. Change of framing, change of energy.
- The transcript on screen must be the actual committed file, not a mock-up. If the project is not yet built when this is filmed, film a different script. The brand rule is that every post has evidence behind it.
- Keep the mock "Tuesday 9am. Booked." clearly labelled as what a naive tool would do, so nobody clips it as the real behaviour.
