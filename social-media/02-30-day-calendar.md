# 2. Thirty-day LinkedIn content calendar

**Account:** Hemayet Hossain, LinkedIn
**Niche:** Salesforce and practical AI for Australian trades and service businesses
**Audience:** owners and operations managers of trades and service businesses (primary); Salesforce partner leads and hiring managers in Sydney (secondary)
**Offer:** Salesforce implementation and org-rescue consulting, Agentforce enablement, small AI tools
**Goal:** qualified inbound conversations

**How to use it.** Day 1 is a Monday. The strategy commits to four posts a week, so
the starred days (★ Mon, Wed, Thu, Fri) are the publishing plan and the
unstarred days are a bank of ready posts for when a starred one is not working,
or for a month when there is capacity for more. Every entry is specific enough
to write from without further research; the source deliverable is named where
one exists.

**Pillars:** BL = The Build Log · FN = Field Notes for Admins · LK = Where Trades Businesses Leak Money · AI = AI That Knows When to Stop
**Mix across the thirty:** 9 educational, 4 entertaining, 5 relatable, 5 authority, 4 community, 3 promotional.

**Disclosure rule.** Every entry that names a fictional company includes "a simulated engagement" or "a business I modelled" in the body. The hooks below assume that line follows within two sentences.

## Checks before scheduling each post

- Does the hook fit above "see more" (about 210 characters)?
- Is the disclosure in the body within two sentences of the fictional name?
- Is there a number, and a link to the evidence in the first comment?
- Is the call to action one word or one question, never "DM me to learn more"?
- Is it repeating a topic from the previous fourteen days? If so, swap with a bank day.
