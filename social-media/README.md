## Assumptions used to fill the blanks

| Blank | Filled with | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Business / personal brand | **Hemayet Hossain**, personal brand, trading as Hossain Consulting | Portfolio header and every repo footer |
| Industry / niche | Salesforce implementation and practical AI tooling for **Australian trades, home-services and service-heavy businesses** | Seven of eight engagements are trades, energy, retail or warranty service businesses |
| Target audience | **Primary:** owners and operations managers of Australian trades and service businesses, 20 to 500 staff, running on spreadsheets, a shared inbox or an unmaintained Salesforce org. **Secondary:** Salesforce partner delivery leads and hiring managers in Sydney. **Amplifiers:** admins and consultants on the same certification path | The scenarios were built for the primary group; the secondary group is who reads a portfolio |
| Main offer | Salesforce implementation and org-rescue consulting, with Agentforce enablement and small AI tools as the second offer | Portfolio subtitle and the Home Services AI track |
| Primary goal | **Leads**, defined as qualified inbound conversations. Brand awareness is the leading indicator, not the goal | A consultant with a new portfolio needs conversations, not an audience |
| Platforms | **LinkedIn** (primary, text, carousel, native video). **YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels** (one short video a week, repurposed from LinkedIn). **GitHub and the portfolio site** as the destination every post points to | The audience and the hiring managers are on LinkedIn; the evidence is on GitHub |
| Publishing capacity | **Four posts a week** on LinkedIn, one of which is a video that is also cut for Shorts and Reels | Fits inside the 8 to 12 hours a week the engagements already take |
| Tone | Plain, specific, first person, Australian English, numbers over adjectives | Matches the deliverables |

## Two house rules that override any template

1. **Disclose the simulation in the post, not in a comment.** Every story that
   names SunRise Solar, Meridian, TradeLink, Coastline, Ironbark or Kurrajong
   says "a simulated engagement" or "a business I modelled" the first time the
   name appears. The portfolio makes this promise in its first thirty seconds;
   the feed must too.
2. **Every post carries a number and a link to the evidence.** A claim without a
   repo, a screenshot or a query behind it does not go out. That is the brand.
