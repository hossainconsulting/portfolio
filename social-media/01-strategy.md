# 1. Social media strategy

**Brand:** Hemayet Hossain, Salesforce implementation consultant, Sydney
**Niche:** Salesforce and practical AI for Australian trades and service businesses
**Goal:** qualified inbound conversations (leads), with awareness as the leading indicator
**Platforms:** LinkedIn (primary), YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels (one video a week), GitHub and the portfolio as the destination
**Capacity:** four posts a week, roughly three hours of production and one hour of engagement

---

## Brand positioning

**One line:** *The Salesforce consultant who shows the working.*

**The longer version, for the LinkedIn About section:**

> I build complete Salesforce implementations for Australian trades and service
> businesses, and I publish every decision, every query and every mistake so you
> can check my thinking before you hire it. The engagements on my portfolio are
> simulations, disclosed as such, built end to end in real orgs. The
> configuration proves the clicks happened. The documents prove the thinking did.

**Why this position and not "Salesforce consultant, Sydney":**

- Most consultants sell outcomes they cannot show because the client work is
  confidential. You can show all of it, because none of it is confidential.
  The disclosure is a strength when it is stated first, and a liability only
  when a reader discovers it later.
- The audience does not know Salesforce and does not want to. They know
  unquoted enquiries, uninvoiced jobs, missed after-hours calls and a customer
  count nobody trusts. Every post leads with one of those and lets the platform
  be the answer, not the subject.
- "Shows the working" is also the AI position. Coverage answers come from a
  lookup, not the model. The booking agent has one write path. The after-hours
  agent escalates a gas leak to a human. The feed makes bounded, checkable AI
  the norm at a time when most AI content promises the opposite.

**What the brand is not:** not a certification-tips account, not a Salesforce
news account, not a "10 ChatGPT prompts" account. Certifications appear as
context, never as content.

## Four content pillars

| # | Pillar | What it is | Job it does | Share of posts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Build Log** | One decision from the current engagement, with the number, the reason and the link to the deliverable. Working in public. | Authority and trust. Proof that the work exists. | 30% |
| 2 | **Field Notes for Admins** | Practical, saveable Salesforce how-tos drawn from small orgs: licences held by users who never logged in, a merge rule that fails on blank phones, two things both called "entitlement". | Educational. Saves and shares. Reaches the amplifier audience, who send it to their managers. | 25% |
| 3 | **Where Trades Businesses Leak Money** | The audience's problem in their words: the enquiry never quoted, the job never invoiced, the 6pm call nobody answered, the customer counted three times. Each ends with what a fix looks like. | Lead generation. This is the pillar that produces DMs. | 30% |
| 4 | **AI That Knows When to Stop** | Bounded AI for service businesses: what the agent is not allowed to say, why the model cannot add up the invoice, what a gas leak does to the conversation. | Differentiation. Nobody else in the niche is posting this. | 15% |

Community and story content is not a fifth pillar. It is a format applied
inside these four: a poll on pillar 2, a "what did you find in week one"
question on pillar 1, a Sydney admins question on pillar 2.

## Formats by platform

### LinkedIn (primary)

| Format | Use it for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Text post, 150 to 250 words** | Pillars 1 and 3. Story, number, decision, link in the first comment. | The workhorse. Three of four weekly posts. Hook in the first two lines before "see more". |
| **Text post with one screenshot** | Pillar 1 and 2 when a table or a query result carries the point. | Screenshot the deliverable table, not the Setup screen. Redact nothing; it is all fictional. |
| **PDF carousel, 8 to 10 slides** | Pillar 2 checklists. | Once a fortnight. Highest save rate of any format. Built in Canva from one template. |
| **Native video, 45 seconds, captioned** | Pillar 4 and the occasional pillar 3 story. | Once a week. Phone, front camera, captions burned in, no music. |
| **Poll** | Community questions inside pillars 1 and 2. | At most twice a month. Answers become next month's posts. |

### YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels (secondary)

| Format | Use it for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Vertical 45-second video** | The same weekly video, re-cut vertical, with a platform-specific caption and five hashtags. | Zero extra filming. Instagram and YouTube exist to be found by owners who are not on LinkedIn, and to give the LinkedIn video a second life. |

### GitHub and the portfolio (destination)

Not a posting platform. Every post links to a repo file or the portfolio in the
first comment. Repo READMEs are written so a visitor arriving from a post
understands the scenario in one screen. That is already true.

## Weekly posting schedule

Four posts, one video, batched in one sitting.

| Day | Slot | Pillar | Format | Time cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sunday** | Batch session | All | Write the week's four posts, record and caption one video, schedule everything | 2.5 hours |
| **Monday, 7:30am AEST** | Field Notes | 2 | Text with screenshot, or carousel every second week | Scheduled |
| **Wednesday, 7:30am** | Leak | 3 | Text story | Scheduled |
| **Thursday, 12:00pm** | AI that knows when to stop | 4 | Native video on LinkedIn; the same cut to Shorts and Reels by 6pm | Scheduled |
| **Friday, 7:30am** | Build Log | 1 | Text with screenshot and the repo link | Scheduled |
| **Every weekday, 15 minutes** | Engagement | All | Reply to every comment within the hour; leave eight substantive comments a week on posts by trades owners, Salesforce partners and Sydney admins | 1.25 hours |

Total: about four hours a week. The engagement slot matters more than a fifth
post. On LinkedIn, comments on other people's posts are where the secondary
audience first sees the name.

Posting times are a starting point. After the first month, move each slot to
the hour the analytics say the audience is online.

## Follower-to-customer journey

Five steps, and each step has one thing that moves a person to the next.

| Step | Where they are | What moves them on | What you measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Sees a post** | A trades owner or a partner delivery lead sees a pillar 3 or pillar 2 post in the feed, usually because someone they follow commented on it. | A hook that names their problem in their words, with a number. | Impressions, reach from comments |
| **2. Follows** | They open the profile. The banner says "Salesforce for trades and service businesses, Sydney". The About section says "I show the working". The featured section has the portfolio and one carousel. | A profile that confirms the post was not a one-off. | Profile views, follower growth |
| **3. Reads the evidence** | They click through to the portfolio or a repo. They see a scenario that looks like their business, a deliverable that looks like something they would want, and a disclosure that tells them the truth in thirty seconds. | The deliverables folder. This is the step no other consultant can offer. | Portfolio clicks (Cloudflare analytics), repo traffic |
| **4. Starts a conversation** | They comment "we've got this exact problem", or DM, or reply to the "comment 'licence' for the query" call to action. | A call to action that costs them one word. Then a reply within the hour that answers properly, not "DM me". | Comments with a problem in them, DMs, people who take the query |
| **5. Books a call** | After a useful exchange, a single sentence: "If you want, I'll do the first-week checks on your org with you. Thirty minutes, no pitch." | A specific, small, free first step that looks like the work itself. | Discovery calls booked, and where they came from |

The secondary audience (partners, hiring managers) follows the same path but
step 5 is a coffee or an interview, not a discovery call. Do not build a
separate funnel for them. They are watching the same posts and drawing the
same conclusion.

## Five measurable monthly goals

Set for month one. Revise after the first thirty days of real numbers.

| # | Goal | Target | Measured where | Why this number |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Publish the plan** | 16 LinkedIn posts, 4 of them video, every one with a number and an evidence link | Post count | Consistency is the whole game in month one. Nothing else moves if this does not. |
| 2 | **Engage outward** | 32 substantive comments on other people's posts, at least half on trades owners' or partners' posts | Weekly tally, kept in a note | Comments are how a new profile gets seen. Eight a week is fifteen minutes a day. |
| 3 | **Grow the right followers** | +150 followers, with at least 40 identifiable as trades or service business people, or Salesforce partner staff, in Australia | LinkedIn analytics, follower list reviewed monthly | Small on purpose. A hundred owners beat a thousand admins for this goal. |
| 4 | **Start conversations** | 6 inbound conversations that name a real problem (a comment describing their org, a DM, a request for the query or template) | Kept in a simple spreadsheet: date, person, source post, what they asked | This is the lead metric. Six in month one is realistic from a standing start. |
| 5 | **Book calls** | 1 discovery call or coffee from social, and the portfolio's monthly visitors up 50% on the month before | Calendar; Cloudflare analytics on portfolio.hossainconsulting.com | One is the honest month-one number. The portfolio traffic is the check that step 3 of the journey is working. |

**What is deliberately not a goal:** likes, viral reach, follower count on
Shorts or Reels. Those are noted, not targeted.

## Execution notes for one person with no budget

- **Batch on Sunday, engage on weekdays.** Never write on the day.
- **One Canva template** for carousels and one for video captions. Set up once.
- **The content is already written.** Every deliverable in the repos is a post.
  The build log is a post a week for as long as the engagements run. The job is
  extraction and framing, not invention.
- **Keep a swipe file** in a single note: every comment that describes a
  reader's problem is next month's pillar 3 post.
- **Read the analytics on the first of the month, for twenty minutes, against
  the five goals. Then stop.** Monthly review, not daily checking.
