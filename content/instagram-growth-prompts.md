# Instagram Growth Prompt Library

Eight reusable prompts for building and running an Instagram growth system
without paid tools or advertising. Each prompt is a fill-in template: replace
every `[BRACKETED]` placeholder before use.

## Placeholder legend

| Placeholder | What to put there |
|---|---|
| `[NICHE]` | The industry or subject the account operates in |
| `[TARGET AUDIENCE]` | Who the account serves |
| `[DESIRED RESULT]` | The outcome the audience is trying to reach |
| `[FOLLOWER COUNT]` | Current follower count |
| `[NUMBER]` | How many posts per week are realistic |
| `[GROWTH/MONETIZATION GOAL]` | The one goal the next 30 days serve |
| `[PILLAR 1]` to `[PILLAR 4]` | The four content pillars |
| `[SPECIFIC TOPIC]` / `[TOPIC]` | One narrow topic, not a category |
| `[SAVE/SHARE/COMMENT/FOLLOW]` | The single action the CTA asks for |
| `[VOICE DESCRIPTION]` | Two or three lines describing the brand voice |
| `[DESIRED ACTION]` | What the reader should do after the post |
| `[AUDIENCE]` | Who the engagement routine targets |

Suggested order of use: 1 → 2 → 3, then 4 to 7 as production prompts, and 8
as the daily operating routine.

---

## 1. Build My Instagram Growth Strategy

> Act as an Instagram growth strategist for the [NICHE] industry. My account
> helps [TARGET AUDIENCE] achieve [DESIRED RESULT]. I currently have
> [FOLLOWER COUNT] followers and can publish [NUMBER] times per week. Create a
> practical 120-day strategy covering positioning, profile optimization,
> content pillars, weekly posting schedule, engagement activities,
> collaboration ideas, and monetization milestones. Divide it into four 30-day
> phases. For every phase, specify objectives, weekly actions, content
> targets, and measurable KPIs. Prioritize methods requiring no paid tools or
> advertising.

**Use when:** starting an account, or resetting one that has stalled.
**Output expected:** four phases, each with objectives, weekly actions,
content targets, and KPIs.

---

## 2. Find Content Ideas My Audience Wants

> Research the likely questions, frustrations, mistakes, objections, and goals
> of [TARGET AUDIENCE] within [NICHE]. Generate 50 highly specific Instagram
> content ideas—not broad topics. Organize them into: beginner questions,
> common mistakes, step-by-step tutorials, myths, personal stories,
> comparisons, case studies, and purchase objections. For each idea, provide a
> compelling post title, recommended format—Reel, carousel, Story, or static
> post—and the exact problem it solves.

**Use when:** the idea backlog is empty or the ideas have drifted generic.
**Output expected:** 50 ideas across eight categories, each with title,
format, and the problem solved.

---

## 3. Create My 30-Day Content Calendar

> Create a 30-day Instagram content calendar for a [NICHE] creator whose goal
> is [GROWTH/MONETIZATION GOAL]. Use these content pillars: [PILLAR 1],
> [PILLAR 2], [PILLAR 3], and [PILLAR 4]. Include one primary post per day and
> three simple Story ideas. For every primary post, provide the topic, format,
> hook, key message, CTA, and funnel stage—awareness, trust, or conversion.
> Balance educational, relatable, authority-building, and promotional content.
> Present everything in a clear table.

**Use when:** the strategy (prompt 1) and idea bank (prompt 2) exist and the
month needs scheduling.
**Output expected:** a 30-row table with three Story ideas per day.

---

## 4. Write High-Retention Reel Scripts

> Write 10 Instagram Reel scripts about [SPECIFIC TOPIC] for
> [TARGET AUDIENCE]. Each Reel should take 20–35 seconds to deliver and
> contain: a pattern-interrupt opening, a specific promise, three concise
> value points, natural transitions, and one clear CTA. Add on-screen text,
> suggested visuals or B-roll, and a caption for each Reel. Use conversational
> language understandable by beginners. Avoid vague advice, exaggerated
> claims, and introductions such as "Hi everyone."

**Use when:** batching a week or two of Reels on one topic.
**Output expected:** ten scripts with on-screen text, visual notes, and
captions.

---

## 5. Turn One Topic Into a Carousel

> Create a 9-slide Instagram carousel about [TOPIC] for [TARGET AUDIENCE].
> Slide 1 must use a curiosity-driven hook. Slide 2 should identify the
> reader's problem. Slides 3–7 should explain a practical step-by-step
> solution with examples. Slide 8 should summarize the action plan. Slide 9
> should include a relevant CTA asking readers to [SAVE/SHARE/COMMENT/FOLLOW].
> Keep each slide under 30 words and include a suggested visual layout plus a
> complete caption.

**Use when:** a topic deserves a save-worthy, step-by-step treatment.
**Output expected:** nine slides under 30 words each, a layout suggestion,
and a caption.

---

## 6. Repurpose One Idea Into 10 Posts

> Repurpose this source material into 10 distinct Instagram posts:
> [PASTE ARTICLE, TRANSCRIPT, NOTES, OR ORIGINAL POST]. Produce three Reels,
> two carousels, two Story sequences, two text posts, and one FAQ post. Give
> every piece a different angle so the ideas do not feel repeated. Include the
> hook, content structure, caption, CTA, and suggested visual. Preserve my
> core message but simplify it for [TARGET AUDIENCE] and match this brand
> voice: [VOICE DESCRIPTION].

**Use when:** a long-form piece (article, talk, client note) already exists.
**Output expected:** ten posts in the stated mix, each with a distinct angle.

---

## 7. Write Captions That Generate Action

> Write five Instagram caption variations for this post:
> [DESCRIBE OR PASTE POST]. My audience is [TARGET AUDIENCE], and I want them
> to [DESIRED ACTION]. Create one story-led caption, one educational caption,
> one contrarian caption, one short caption, and one authority-building
> caption. Each version must include a strong first line, useful specifics,
> short readable paragraphs, a natural CTA, and 5–8 relevant searchable
> keywords. Do not use filler, engagement bait, or unsupported claims.

**Use when:** the visual is ready and the caption is the last step.
**Output expected:** five captions in five registers, each with keywords.

---

## 8. Create a Daily Engagement System

> Design a 30-minute daily Instagram engagement routine for a [NICHE] creator
> targeting [AUDIENCE]. Divide it into actions to complete before posting,
> immediately after posting, and later in the day. Include methods for finding
> relevant accounts, writing meaningful comments, responding to Stories,
> handling DMs, starting collaborations, and converting conversations without
> sounding salesy. Provide ready-to-edit templates for five comments, five
> Story replies, three networking DMs, and three follow-up messages.

**Use when:** setting up the daily operating rhythm that makes posting
compound.
**Output expected:** a three-block routine plus 16 editable message
templates.
