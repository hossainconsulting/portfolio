# YouTube Growth Prompt Library

Eight reusable prompts for building and running a faceless or AI-assisted
YouTube channel. Each prompt is a fill-in template: replace every
`[BRACKETED]` placeholder before use.

## Placeholder legend

| Placeholder | What to put there |
|---|---|
| `[NICHE OR TOPIC]` / `[NICHE]` | The subject the channel covers |
| `[TARGET AUDIENCE]` / `[AUDIENCE]` / `[TARGET VIEWER]` | Who the channel serves |
| `[VIEWS / SUBSCRIBERS / LEADS / SALES]` | The one goal the next 30 days serve |
| `[NUMBER]` | How many videos per week are realistic |
| `[VIDEO TOPIC]` | One narrow topic, not a category |
| `[8–12 MINUTES]` | Target runtime for the script |
| `[MAIN RESULT OR ANGLE]` | The outcome or story the video delivers |
| `[PASTE SCRIPT]` | The full script from prompt 4 |
| `[CLAUDE / CHATGPT / CANVA / CAPCUT / ELEVENLABS / OTHER TOOLS]` | The tools actually available |
| `[TITLE]` | The final title chosen from prompt 5 |
| `[PASTE VIDEO TITLES, VIEWS, ...]` | Exported analytics for recent videos |

Suggested order of use: 1 → 2 → 3 to plan, 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 to produce and
publish each video, and 8 after every batch to decide what comes next.

## 1. Find a YouTube Niche With Viral Potential

> Act as a YouTube channel strategist. I want to build a faceless/AI-assisted
> YouTube channel in the niche [NICHE OR TOPIC] for the audience
> [TARGET AUDIENCE].
>
> Analyze this niche and identify 5 content sub-niches that have strong
> potential for repeatable videos, high curiosity, and shareability.
>
> For each sub-niche, give me:
> - Target viewer
> - Main viewer problem/desire
> - Best video format
> - 5 example video ideas
> - Why people would click/watch
> - Monetization possibilities
> - Difficulty level: Easy / Medium / Hard
>
> Finally, recommend the ONE sub-niche I should focus on for the next 30 days
> and explain why. Avoid generic suggestions.

**Use when:** choosing or narrowing a channel's focus.
**Output expected:** five sub-niches with seven attributes each, and one
recommendation with reasoning.

---

## 2. Build My 30-Day YouTube Content Plan

> You are managing my YouTube content strategy for the next 30 days. My
> channel is about [NICHE], targeting [AUDIENCE], and my main goal is
> [VIEWS / SUBSCRIBERS / LEADS / SALES].
>
> Create a 30-day publishing plan with [NUMBER] videos per week.
>
> For every video include:
> 1. Video topic
> 2. Clickable working title
> 3. Main hook/angle
> 4. Target viewer
> 5. Video format
> 6. Estimated length
> 7. Why this topic could perform
> 8. Related follow-up video
>
> Use a mix of evergreen, trending, curiosity-driven, searchable, and highly
> shareable topics. Make the ideas specific enough that I can immediately
> create them.

**Use when:** the sub-niche (prompt 1) is chosen and the month needs a
schedule.
**Output expected:** a full month of videos, each with eight fields.

## 3. Generate High-Potential Video Ideas

> Act as a YouTube viral-idea researcher for a channel about [NICHE].
> Generate 30 highly specific video ideas for [TARGET AUDIENCE].
>
> Each idea must trigger at least one of these: curiosity, surprise, fear of
> missing out, transformation, controversy, usefulness, comparison, or
> discovery.
>
> For every idea provide:
> - Video concept
> - 3 possible titles
> - Main curiosity gap
> - Opening hook
> - Why the viewer would keep watching
> - Best format: tutorial / list / story / experiment / comparison /
>   documentary / reaction
>
> Avoid broad ideas like "10 tips for [topic]." Prioritize concepts with a
> clear story, outcome, experiment, discovery, or strong promise.

**Use when:** the idea backlog is thin or the ideas have drifted generic.
**Output expected:** 30 ideas, each with three titles, a curiosity gap, a
hook, a retention reason, and a format.

---

## 4. Turn Any Idea Into a High-Retention Script

> Write a YouTube script about [VIDEO TOPIC] for [TARGET AUDIENCE]. Target
> length: [8–12 MINUTES].
>
> Structure it for audience retention:
> - First 5 seconds: powerful pattern-interrupt hook
> - 5–30 seconds: explain what viewers will discover without giving
>   everything away
> - Main sections: introduce a new insight, example, question, or surprise
>   regularly
> - Use simple conversational language
> - Include specific examples instead of vague advice
> - Add natural transitions between sections
> - Remove unnecessary introductions and filler
> - Build toward the strongest insight/payoff near the end
> - End with a natural reason to watch another related video
>
> Also mark useful editing notes like [B-ROLL], [SCREEN RECORDING],
> [TEXT ON SCREEN], and [VISUAL EXAMPLE].

**Use when:** a topic from prompt 2 or 3 is ready to be written.
**Output expected:** a full script with inline editing markers.

## 5. Create the Title + Thumbnail Packaging

> Act as a YouTube packaging expert. My video is about [VIDEO TOPIC], and the
> main outcome/story is [MAIN RESULT OR ANGLE]. My audience is
> [TARGET VIEWER].
>
> Create 15 YouTube title + thumbnail combinations.
>
> Requirements:
> - Titles should create curiosity without misleading viewers
> - Keep most titles under approximately 60 characters
> - Don't repeat the full title on the thumbnail
> - Thumbnail text should preferably be 1–4 words
> - Give each thumbnail ONE obvious visual focal point
> - Make the title and thumbnail create an unanswered question together
>
> For each option provide:
>
> Title:
> Thumbnail text:
> Thumbnail visual:
> Curiosity trigger:
>
> Then rank the top 3 combinations and explain why they are strongest.

**Use when:** the script exists and the video needs its click package.
**Output expected:** 15 combinations in the four-line format, then a
ranked top three.

## 6. Turn the Script Into an AI Production Workflow

> Convert the following YouTube script into a complete production plan that a
> beginner can execute using AI tools.
>
> SCRIPT:
> [PASTE SCRIPT]
>
> Break the video into scenes. For each scene provide:
> - Voiceover line
> - Visual needed
> - B-roll/search keywords
> - AI image/video prompt when appropriate
> - On-screen text
> - Animation or zoom suggestion
> - Sound effect/music suggestion
>
> Keep visuals changing frequently enough to maintain attention without
> making the video chaotic.
>
> At the end, give me a simple workflow showing what I should create using
> [CLAUDE / CHATGPT / CANVA / CAPCUT / ELEVENLABS / OTHER TOOLS] and the exact
> order I should complete the tasks.

**Use when:** the script is final and production starts.
**Output expected:** a scene-by-scene shot list plus an ordered tool
workflow.

## 7. Optimize Every Video Before Publishing

> Act as my YouTube publishing assistant. I am uploading a video about
> [VIDEO TOPIC] with this title: [TITLE].
>
> Create my complete upload package:
> - Optimized YouTube description
> - Strong first 2 description lines
> - Relevant keywords/topics
> - Chapters with useful chapter names
> - 3 pinned-comment options designed to encourage discussion
> - 5 community-post ideas promoting the video
> - 5 YouTube Shorts ideas that can promote the long-form video
> - Suggested related video I should link viewers to next
>
> Keep everything natural and viewer-focused. Do not keyword-stuff or write
> robotic SEO copy.

**Use when:** the video is rendered and ready to upload.
**Output expected:** description, keywords, chapters, pinned comments,
community posts, Shorts ideas, and an end-screen link.

## 8. Let Claude Analyze Results and Decide What to Make Next

> You are my YouTube growth analyst. I will give you performance data from my
> recent videos.
>
> DATA:
> [PASTE VIDEO TITLES, VIEWS, IMPRESSIONS, CTR, AVERAGE VIEW DURATION,
> RETENTION, SUBSCRIBERS GAINED, TRAFFIC SOURCES, ETC.]
>
> Analyze the data and tell me:
> 1. Which videos significantly outperformed
> 2. Which topics/angles repeatedly worked
> 3. Which titles or thumbnails may be limiting CTR
> 4. Which videos have strong clicks but weak retention
> 5. Which videos have good retention but weak distribution
> 6. Patterns I should stop repeating
> 7. Topics/formats I should double down on
>
> Then create my next 10 video ideas based specifically on these findings.
> For each idea, explain which previous performance signal supports the
> recommendation.
>
> Finish with my 3 highest-priority actions for the next 7 days.

**Use when:** a batch of videos has been live long enough to have data.
**Output expected:** seven diagnostic answers, ten evidence-backed ideas,
and three priority actions.
