# Instagram (and Threads)

The account exists (`@hossainconsulting`, Crows Nest NSW). Client-facing:
trades business owners are on Instagram far more than on LinkedIn. Content is
carousels and Reels, both cut from the pipeline.

## Profile

| Field | Set to |
|---|---|
| Account type | Professional → Business, category "Consulting agency" |
| Name field (searchable) | Hemayet Hossain · Salesforce Consultant Sydney |
| Bio (150) | Brand kit → Instagram bio |
| Link | `/links` with `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=profile` |
| Contact buttons | Email, and the booking link |
| Highlights | Projects · Certifications · Agentforce · About (each with a cover in the site palette) |
| Pinned | The best carousel, the "what this is" Reel, the disclosure post |

## Content

- **Carousels, 2 a week, 1080×1350.** Slide 1 headline, slide 2 the problem
  in the client's words, slides 3–8 the build (one decision or one screenshot
  per slide), last slide "full write-up: link in bio" with the disclosure.
  Alt text on every slide (Instagram indexes it).
- **Reels, 2 a week.** The same vertical video as TikTok and Shorts. Remove
  the TikTok watermark; Instagram demotes it.
- Caption: first line is the hook, then 3–5 sentences, then the disclosure
  line, then 5 hashtags. Keywords in the caption count for Instagram search.
- Location tag: Sydney or Crows Nest on posts aimed at local clients.

## Threads

Free with the account. Mirror the X thread. Threads posts are indexed by
Google; X posts mostly are not. Same handle, same bio, link to the hub.

## Cadence

2 carousels + 2 Reels a week, 4 Stories a week (behind-the-scenes of the
build, a poll, a repost of a LinkedIn post as a screenshot with a link
sticker).

## Metrics

Reach, profile visits, link clicks, follows from Sydney. Meta Business Suite,
monthly.
