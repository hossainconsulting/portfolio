## Content

**Long-form, every second week, 8–15 minutes.** Screen recording of the org
with voice. Chaptered. Structure:

1. The requirement in the client's words (0:00)
2. The decision and the alternatives rejected (1:00)
3. The build, sped up where it is clicking (3:00)
4. The test (9:00)
5. What went wrong or what would break it (11:00)
6. Disclosure and the hub (12:30)

Title starts with the outcome: "Entitlements that miss a regulated deadline
escalate themselves — Service Cloud milestone build". Under 70 characters.

Description: first two lines are the summary (shown before "more"). Then
chapters, then the repo link, then the hub link with UTM, then the disclosure.

Thumbnail: 1280×720, the site palette, four words maximum, no face required.

**Shorts, twice a week, 45–90 s.** The same file uploaded to TikTok and Reels.
Caption burned in. First two seconds state the problem.

Playlists: one per engagement, one per certification, one for AI tooling.

## Metrics

Watch time, subscribers, traffic sources (search vs. suggested vs. external),
click-through rate on thumbnails. Monthly.
