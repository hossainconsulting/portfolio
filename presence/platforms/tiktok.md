## Content

- **Two a week, 45–90 seconds, vertical.** Screen recording with voice.
  Caption burned in (most watch muted). First two seconds are the problem in
  the client's words: "Your plumbers text the office to find out where their
  next job is."
- Pillars 3 and 5 do best here (AI for small business, plain-language
  trades questions). Certification content does not.
- Series work: "Fixing a trades business's CRM, part N".
- Caption: hook, disclosure short form, 4 hashtags. TikTok search reads
  captions and on-screen text.
- Reply to comments with a video when the question is good; that is the
  cheapest content there is.
