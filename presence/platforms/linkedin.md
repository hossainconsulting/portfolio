## Content shape

- **3 posts a week** from the pipeline: Monday the takeaway, Wednesday the
  picture, Friday the lesson.
- First line under 12 words and it is the whole point. LinkedIn truncates at
  about 210 characters; the hook has to land before that.
- Line breaks every one or two sentences. No walls.
- Link in the first comment, not the body. Body links get less reach.
- Document posts (upload the carousel PDF) get the most dwell time; use one
  a week.
- 3 to 5 hashtags at the end. From the brand kit sets.
- Reply to every comment within a day; the algorithm rewards the first hour.
- **Never** engagement bait ("agree?"), never "humbled", never a screenshot
  of your own certification without a lesson attached to it.

## Company page cadence

One reshare a week of the best personal post, with a one-line frame. Plus any
hub release note.

## Recruiter-specific

- Turn on "Open to work" recruiter-only. The green frame is for people who
  want any job; the recruiter filter is for people who want a specific one.
- Skills assessments: take the Salesforce one if it is offered; the badge
  shows in recruiter search.
- Respond to every recruiter message even to decline. They keep notes.

## Client-specific

- The Services page and a "Book a call" link (Calendly or similar) in Featured.
- Recommendations: ask one per finished engagement equivalent, from a peer
  who reviewed the work. Never invent one for a fictional stakeholder.

## Metrics

Profile views, search appearances, post impressions, followers. Monthly, into
`measurement.md`.
