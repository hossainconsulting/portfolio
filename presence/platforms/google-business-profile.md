# Google Business Profile

The client-side search surface. "Salesforce consultant near me", "CRM
consultant Crows Nest", and the Maps panel that appears for a branded search.
It also feeds the knowledge panel and Google's AI answers.

## Eligibility

A service-area business qualifies if it serves customers at their location
and has a real operating base. Hossain Consulting does. Hide the address, list
the service areas.

## Setup

| Field | Set to |
|---|---|
| Business name | Hossain Consulting (no keywords appended; that is a suspension risk) |
| Primary category | Software company |
| Additional categories | Business management consultant · Computer consultant · Business to business service |
| Address | Entered for verification, then **hidden** ("I deliver goods and services to my customers") |
| Service areas | Crows Nest NSW, North Sydney, Sydney, Greater Sydney, New South Wales |
| Phone | A business number, not personal. Same number on Bing Places, LinkedIn, ABN lookup. |
| Website | Company site once the apex is fixed; hub until then. UTM `utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=profile`. |
| Hours | Mon–Fri 9:00–17:30 |
| Description (750) | Brand kit → GBP description |
| Opening date | The ABN registration month |
| Services | One per keyword group: Salesforce administration · Sales Cloud implementation · Service Cloud implementation · Agentforce agent build · Data model and security design · Salesforce automation (Flow) · Reporting and dashboards · AI tooling for small business. Each with a 300-char description. |
| Attributes | Online appointments · Identifies as… (only if true) |
| Social profiles | LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, X, Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest (Google supports these seven) |
| Photos | Logo, cover (headshot or banner), 3 diagram images from the deliverables, 1 workspace photo. Minimum 5. |
| Booking link | Calendly or equivalent |
| Q&A | Seed three questions yourself and answer them: "Do you work with small trades businesses?", "Are the portfolio projects real clients?", "Do you work remotely?" |

## Verification

Video verification is the usual path for a service-area business: film the
workspace, business signage or documents, and something proving you operate
there. Postcard is the fallback and takes two weeks.

## Cadence

- One **Update** post a week: a photo, 100 words, a "Learn more" button to
  the hub. Posts expire from prominence after 7 days.
- Reply to every review within 24 hours. Ask for reviews only from people who
  actually worked with you; a review from a fictional stakeholder is fraud.
- Check the Performance tab monthly: searches (direct vs. discovery), views,
  website clicks, calls, direction requests.

## Risks

- Google suspends profiles with keyword-stuffed names or virtual-office
  addresses. Use the real name and the real base.
- Do not create a second profile for the person. One profile, the business.
