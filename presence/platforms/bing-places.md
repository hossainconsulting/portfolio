# Bing Places for Business (Microsoft search)

Bing, Copilot, Windows search, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo, Ecosia and Apple's Siri
suggestions all draw on Bing's index. Bing Places is the Microsoft equivalent
of Google Business Profile, and it will import GBP wholesale.

## Setup

1. https://www.bingplaces.com → Sign in with a Microsoft account (create one on
   the business email).
2. **Import from Google** → authorise → select the Hossain Consulting listing.
3. Review every imported field against `google-business-profile.md`. Fix the
   category mapping (Bing's categories differ).
4. Turn on **periodic sync** so GBP edits flow through.
5. Verify (usually auto-verified after the Google import; otherwise phone or
   email).

## Bing Webmaster Tools

Separate product, same account. Import the site from Google Search Console
(see `../seo.md` §4). Submit the sitemap. Enable IndexNow.

## Cadence

None beyond the GBP sync. Check the dashboard monthly for impressions and
clicks.

## Apple Business Connect (optional, same data)

https://businessconnect.apple.com. Apple Maps and Siri. Enter the same NAP,
category and hours. Verify by phone. No further maintenance.
