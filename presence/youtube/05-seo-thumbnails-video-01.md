# 5. SEO and thumbnail strategy: video 1

Topic: the five security-model questions people fail on the Salesforce
Administrator exam, shown on a real org. Niche: Salesforce admin
certification.

## Title (under 60 characters, keyword first)

```
Salesforce Admin Exam: 5 Security Questions People Fail
```

55 characters. The keyword phrase "Salesforce Admin Exam" opens it. The
draft in the roadmap had "(real org)" on the end; that moves to the
thumbnail, where it does more work.

## Three thumbnail concepts

All three use the locked system: paper background, ink text, one green
accent, sans type, no faces. 1280 × 720, text at least 90 px tall, safe
from the timestamp corner. Built from `presence/infographics/system.css`
tokens.

| # | Emotional trigger | Visual | Text overlay (≤ 4 words) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Fear of failing** | A record card with a red "Insufficient privileges" bar across it | FAIL THIS. FAIL EXAM. |
| B | **Curiosity (the disappearing record)** | The same opportunity card twice, one solid, one ghosted at 30% with a green arrow between them | ONE SETTING. GONE. |
| C | **Relief (finally shown, not told)** | A Setup screenshot with five green ticks down the left, a mono "REAL ORG" tag top right | 5 QUESTIONS. REAL ORG. |

Prediction: B has the highest CTR because it shows the hook; C has the
best AVD because it sets the right expectation. Launch with B.

## A/B test plan (YouTube's "Test & compare")

Round 1, first 7 days: B vs C. Metric: watch time share, which is what
YouTube optimises the test on.

Round 2, if B wins: B vs B-variant with the text "GONE." only and the
ghosted card larger. Tests whether fewer words raise CTR.

Round 3, at 30 days: winner vs A. Fear thumbnails often win short term and
lose on AVD; this round checks whether the sub-count from A is worth it.

Record each round's CTR and AVD in the monthly review.
