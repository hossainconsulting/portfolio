## The MrBeast playbook, translated

The principles below are from public reporting of the MrBeast production
onboarding document (2024) and his interviews. His model is general
entertainment with seven-figure budgets. Almost none of the tactics
transfer to a one-person B2B channel. The principles do.

| Principle | What it means for this channel |
|---|---|
| **Title and thumbnail first, then make the video.** The idea is only as good as its packaging. | Every video on the roadmap has its title and thumbnail concept written before recording. If the title cannot be made clickable without lying, the video is not made. |
| **Three numbers: CTR, AVD, AVV.** Click-through rate, average view duration, average view velocity. Everything else is vanity. | Monthly review reports these three per video. Target after 10 videos: CTR 5%, AVD 50%, and each video beating the previous one's first-48-hour views. |
| **The first minute decides.** State the promise, prove you can deliver it, remove every reason to leave. | Scripts open with the failing thing on screen inside 10 seconds, the promise by 30, the first payoff by 60. No intros, no logos, no "in this video". |
| **Re-hook at 1, 3 and 10 minutes.** Viewers decide again at each mark. | The retention plan puts a format change at every 60 to 90 seconds and a forward-looking line at 30%, 50%, 70%. |
| **Never make the viewer wait.** Cut the setup, deliver, then explain. | Show the result, then how. The org walkthrough starts at the broken report, not at Setup. |
| **Consistency beats intensity.** Same format, same schedule, for years. | One long-form every Tuesday for the 12-week launch, then fortnightly; two Shorts a week throughout. Same cold-open format every time. |
| **Wow factor.** Something the viewer has not seen. | Ours is not spectacle. It is the whole org and every decision in a public repo, and showing the failure before the fix. Nobody in the niche does that. |
| **The best idea wins, whoever had it.** | Ideas go through `/presence-research` scoring before they go on the roadmap. The scoreboard decides, not the calendar. |

What does not transfer, and is not attempted: giveaways, stunts, paid
reach, reaction faces, a team. This channel wins on being the only one
showing complete builds, not on being loud.

## How the plan connects to the rest of the presence

- Long-form videos are the pillar source in `presence/content-engine.md`;
  every one is cut into the LinkedIn post, the thread, the carousel and the
  Shorts.
- Video copy passes `presence/writing-checklist.md` before recording.
- Thumbnails use `presence/infographics/system.css` tokens so the channel
  matches the hub and the LinkedIn boards.
- Every video description links the repository and the hub with the UTM
  convention in `presence/measurement.md`.
