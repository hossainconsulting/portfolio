# 8. Viral ideation: ten ideas engineered for shares

Niche: Salesforce implementation for trades businesses, and the admins who
build it. Competitors analysed (admin side, chosen by me; replace if you
follow others): **Salesforce Ben**, **Apex Hours**, **Bradley Rice /
Talent Stacker**.

## The emotional trigger behind each competitor's top-performing content

| Channel | What their best videos do | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Salesforce Ben | "X things every admin should know", release-feature roundups, certification guides | **Fear of missing out** (a feature or a fact everyone else knows) |
| Apex Hours | Long live sessions on one technical topic, exam prep marathons | **Aspiration** (mastery, the community of people who show up) |
| Bradley Rice / Talent Stacker | Career change into Salesforce, salary numbers, "how I got the job" | **Aspiration with a number** (the salary, the timeline), and **fear** (being left behind in a first career) |

None of them show a failure on screen. That is the open lane, and the
angles below use their triggers without their formats.

## Ten ideas

| # | Idea | Trigger | One-line hook (open loop, ≤ 10 words) | Series |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The agent told a customer their warranty was void. It wasn't. Two root causes, shown in the transcript and the config. | Fear | "The AI was confident. The AI was wrong. Twice." | **Yes: "Org Under Load"**, one incident per episode |
| 2 | The quarter a solar company missed with no explanation, traced to one assignment rule nobody had opened in a year. | Curiosity | "Nobody could explain the missed quarter. One rule could." | Yes: Org Under Load |
| 3 | I put a $38,400 opportunity in front of a user who should not see it, and changed one setting at a time until she couldn't. | Curiosity | "One setting. Watch the record disappear." | Yes: "Watch It Happen", one exam topic per episode |
| 4 | What Salesforce actually costs a 14-person plumbing business, in dollars, and the three cases where the honest answer is "not yet". | Controversy (a consultant saying not to buy) | "A Salesforce consultant telling tradies not to buy Salesforce." | Standalone; the reply video is "and here is when you should" |
| 5 | I published seven complete Salesforce builds. Recruiters asked about one thing: the failures. | Aspiration | "Seven builds published. One question from every recruiter." | Standalone, the channel's anchor |
| 6 | 340 duplicate accounts and the merge plan the sales team refused, then accepted, and what changed their minds. | Controversy (people, not config) | "The sales team refused the merge. Here's what changed." | Yes: Org Under Load |
| 7 | An after-hours agent books a job at 7 pm, then gets a call about a gas smell and refuses to book it. On purpose. | Fear (liability) | "The AI refused the job. That's the feature." | Yes: "Agents That Refuse", one guardrail per episode |
| 8 | Three things "entitlement" means in Salesforce, and the data model that made an agent conflate two of them. | Curiosity | "One word. Two objects. One broken agent." | Watch It Happen |
| 9 | A regulated response deadline missed because the SLA lived in a spreadsheet, and the milestone that would have fired. | Fear (regulatory) | "The ombudsman deadline was in a spreadsheet. It missed." | Org Under Load |
| 10 | I let Claude write the invoice. I did not let it do the arithmetic. Here is why. | Curiosity | "The model wrote the invoice. It never touched the numbers." | Agents That Refuse |

Series potential: seven of ten. Three series: **Org Under Load**
(incidents), **Watch It Happen** (exam topics as behaviour), **Agents That
Refuse** (guardrails). Each series gets a playlist, a thumbnail variant
and a consistent cold open so a viewer who liked one recognises the next.

## What makes these shareable rather than merely watchable

- Each one has a moment a viewer can describe in a sentence to a
  colleague ("the AI refused the gas job").
- Each one names a number or a specific object, so it reads as evidence
  rather than opinion.
- None of them is a listicle. The competitors own that format; competing
  on it is competing on their terms.
- Every one carries the disclosure. It is part of the share: "and he
  publishes the whole org".
