# 7. Monetisation roadmap, zero to 100K

Niche: Salesforce implementation and AI tooling for Australian trades and
service businesses. Current subscribers: 0. AdSense is not ranked first;
it is the lagging indicator that confirms the others are working.

## Five revenue streams, ranked by speed of return

| Rank | Stream | First money expected | Unlock milestone | Why it is this fast |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Implementation consulting** (a trades or service business hires Hossain Consulting) | Month 2–4 | 0 subscribers. Needs one owner who watched video 3 or 10 and booked a call. | The channel is a proof-of-work portfolio; one engagement pays more than a year of AdSense at 100K. |
| 2 | **Recruiter-driven contract or role** (the recruiter audience) | Month 2–6 | 0 subscribers. Needs the week-12 video to exist. | Not a channel revenue line in the usual sense, but it is the reason the channel exists and it pays first. Track it. |

| 4 | **Sponsorship and affiliate** (job-management apps, practice-exam providers, Salesforce consultancies hiring) | Month 6–12 | 3,000 to 5,000 subscribers with AVD over 50% | Niche B2B sponsors pay on audience quality, not size; the trades and cert audiences are both buyers. |
| 5 | **AdSense** | Month 6+ | 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 public watch hours (YouTube Partner Program) | High CPM niche, but at this scale it is hundreds of dollars a month, not thousands. |

## Brand deal pitch (first outreach, at 3,000)

Target: an Australian job-management app that serves trades businesses
outgrowing their first tool, or a Salesforce practice-exam provider. Lead
with audience value.

> Subject: Your customers ask me the question your product answers
>
> I run a YouTube channel for Salesforce admins and Australian trades
> business owners. Two of the last ten videos were about the moment a
> plumbing business outgrows its job app; between them they have [n] views,
> [x]% from search, and the comments are owners asking what to move to.
>
> I would like to make one video answering that properly, with your product
> as the worked example, on the condition that I show its limits as well as
> its strengths. My audience trusts the channel because every build is
> published in full, including what broke, and a sponsored video has to
> keep that.
>
> What I can offer: one integrated 10-minute video, a Short, a LinkedIn
> post and a pinned comment, with the video living in the "trades business
> decisions" playlist permanently. What I need from you: a sandbox account
> and a fee of [amount], or an affiliate arrangement at [rate] if you would
> rather start there.
>
> The channel, and the repositories behind it:
> portfolio.hossainconsulting.com

No subscriber count in the pitch until it is over 10,000. Views, search
share and comment quality are the numbers that matter to a niche sponsor.

## Digital product: the Exam-in-Org Kit

**What it is:** for each certification track already covered by the
engagements (Administrator, Advanced Administrator, Agentforce Specialist,
Service Cloud, Sales Cloud), a kit containing: the seed-data script that
sets up a Developer org to the state shown in the videos, a 40-item
"watch it happen" checklist (each item is a setting to change and the
behaviour to observe), the diagrams from the videos as printable PDFs,
and the five-question format for every section of the exam guide.

**Why it fits the pillars:** it is pillar 2 (exam topics in a real org)
made repeatable by the buyer, on the same orgs as pillar 1. It does not
compete with practice exams; it complements them, which is why an exam
provider is also a sponsor prospect.

**Price:** low enough to buy without asking anyone (AUD 39 per track,
AUD 129 for all five). **Delivery:** a private repository invite plus PDFs,
via a simple checkout; no platform with a monthly fee until sales justify
it. **Launch:** at 1,000 subscribers, to the email list, with the week-12
video as the announcement.
