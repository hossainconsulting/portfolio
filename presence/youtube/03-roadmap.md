# 3. Ninety-day content roadmap

Twelve long-form videos, one a week for the launch sprint, plus two Shorts
a week cut from each. After day 90 the long-form cadence drops to
fortnightly as set in `presence/content-engine.md`. Titles are drafts; the
final title is decided by the thumbnail test in `05-seo-thumbnails-*.md`
for each video.

## Upload schedule

| Slot | Day | Format | Time (AEST) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long-form | Tuesday | 8–14 min screen recording of the org, voice over, chaptered | 06:00, so it is live for Sydney lunch and US Monday evening |
| Short 1 | Thursday | 45–90 s cut from Tuesday's video (the failure) | 07:00 |
| Short 2 | Saturday | 45–90 s cut from Tuesday's video (the fix or the rule) | 09:00 |

Budget: about six hours a week during the sprint (record 2, edit 2, cut
Shorts 1, package 1). It is above the four hours in the content engine;
the sprint is the exception.

## Authority progression

- **Weeks 1–2 (credibility):** exam-topic videos on a real org. Cheap to
  make, searched constantly, and they prove the org is real. They earn the
  right to be believed in week 3.
- **Weeks 3–5 (the client audience arrives):** the first trades-owner
  video, then a story with a number (a lost quarter), then the fix. The
  admin audience sees a consultant, not a tutorial channel.
- **Weeks 6–7 (the differentiator):** the agent incident and the rule
  behind it. This is the pair nobody else can make; it becomes the pinned
  video and the LinkedIn Featured item.
- **Weeks 8–11 (depth):** duplicates, entitlements, SLA milestones. Each
  references an earlier video, so the playlist is a course.
- **Week 12 (the claim):** the retrospective. By now the claim "seven
  complete builds, published in full, including what broke" has eleven
  videos of evidence behind it. This is the video recruiters get sent.

## What is measured

Per video, at 7 and 28 days: CTR, AVD, first-48-hour views, traffic
source split (search vs suggested vs external). The sprint is judged at
day 90 on whether search impressions for "salesforce admin exam" and
"salesforce for tradies" exist at all, not on subscriber count.
