# 1. Niche analysis

Ten niches with high CPM, low competition and evergreen demand, scored for
someone starting from zero in Australia. CPM figures are 2025–2026
estimates in USD from published creator reports and are **approximate**;
YouTube Studio will show the real number once the channel is monetised.
Competition is judged on how many channels over 100K subscribers already
own the search results.

## Why this channel's niche ranks first

- CPM is upper-middle because the audience is B2B decision-makers and
  employed professionals; advertisers pay for both.
- Competition is low in the specific intersection. Salesforce tutorial
  channels exist; Salesforce-for-Australian-trades channels do not.
  Certification channels exist; ones that show a complete engagement in a
  real org do not.
- Evergreen: the exam objectives and the "should we get a CRM" question
  recur every year.
- The two non-AdSense paths are already real: consulting work from the
  client audience and recruiter attention from the admin audience. Both
  pay before AdSense ever will.

## What would change the ranking

- If Salesforce stops offering free Developer orgs, difficulty rises
  sharply.
- If the AI-agents niche (rank 2) keeps its CPM as the tools stabilise, it
  becomes the stronger primary niche and this channel is already positioned
  to pivot the Agentforce and Claude pillar into it.
