# Audit 3: Social audit

**Purpose:** check whether the business's socials help a customer decide to enquire.
**Inputs:** profile links (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube as relevant). Public view only. Insights or analytics only if the client gives you access.

## Profile
1. Name, handle and photo match across platforms and the website.
2. Bio says what they do, where, and how to enquire, in the first line.
3. Link in bio goes to a page that works on mobile (quote, booking or contact).
4. Contact button and category are set correctly.

## Content
5. Posted in the last 30 days (record the date of the last post).
6. A mix: real jobs (before and after), people, answers to common questions, offers.
7. Posts have a clear next step, not only a photo.
8. Real photos and video of their own work, not stock images passed off as their own.

## Engagement
9. Comments and messages get answered (note how long the last three took, from public timestamps).
10. Reviews or recommendations are visible and answered.

## Consistency
11. Posting rhythm is realistic for one person (for example 2 a week).
12. Tone matches the business and the brand kit, no copy-paste of unrelated templates.

## Red flags
Dormant for months. Wrong phone number. Unanswered comments. Link in bio leads to an error. Mixed branding.

## Quick-win bank
Rewrite the first line of the bio. Fix the link. Pin one best job. Plan 8 posts from a month of jobs (one photo each, three-line caption). Reply to all open comments.

## Never claim
Follower growth forecasts, "algorithm" tips presented as facts, or numbers you did not see.
