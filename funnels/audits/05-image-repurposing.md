# Audit 5: Image repurposing audit

**Purpose:** find photos the business already owns that can work harder on the website, socials and Google profile.
**Inputs:** a folder or album the client shares, or their Instagram/Facebook. Check permission: people in photos, customers' homes and vehicle plates.

## Inventory
1. Count usable job photos, team photos and before-and-afters. Note the date range.
2. Mark each as: reuse as-is, crop or edit, or do not use (blurry, private, wrong).

## Quality and rights
3. Photos are sharp, well lit and show the work clearly.
4. Customer consent is in place for faces, addresses, licence plates and house numbers. If unsure, mark "do not use".
5. The business owns the photos or has the right to use them.
6. No stock images presented as the business's own work.

## Reuse
7. A before-and-after pair exists for each main service.
8. At least 5 photos from the last 6 months are on the Google Business Profile.
9. The website uses real photos on the main service pages.
10. Each photo has plain-language alt text on the website.
11. Image sizes suit each channel (square, vertical, wide) and files load fast.
12. A simple naming and folder system exists so photos can be found later.

## Output
A reuse table: photo set, where to use it (GBP, website, post, carousel), edit needed, consent status. Plus three quick wins.

## Quick-win bank
Pick 5 for the GBP. Make 3 before-and-after posts. Add alt text to the top 5 website images. Add a "photo consent" line to job paperwork.

## AI images
Use AI images only for abstract or illustrative graphics, and label them. Never present AI images as real work or real results.
