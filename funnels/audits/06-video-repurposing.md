# Audit 6: Video repurposing audit

**Purpose:** find existing video, or video-worthy moments, that can become short clips for several channels.
**Inputs:** links to videos the client already has (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, phone clips), or a list of jobs and routine tasks that could be filmed.

## Inventory
1. List each video: length, topic, date, where it lives, views (only what the client shows you).
2. Mark moments worth cutting: a tip, a demonstration, a before-and-after, a customer answer.

## Checks
3. Each video has a clear first 3 seconds (a hook).
4. Captions exist (most people watch on mute).
5. The audio is clear.
6. Each video has one next step (call, book, link).
7. A vertical version exists or can be cut from the original.
8. Permission is in place for people in the clips (faces, voices, houses, plates).
9. The business owns or may use any music.
10. Titles and descriptions say what the video shows and where.
11. Videos are on the website where relevant (service pages), not only on a social platform.
12. A realistic filming routine exists for one person (for example 2 short clips a week).

## Output
A cut list: source video, clip idea (15-45 s), channels, edit needed. Plus three quick wins.

## Quick-win bank
Cut the best video into three clips. Add captions. Add one video to the top service page. Film a 30-second answer to the most common customer question.

## AI avatars and clones
Use an AI version of the client only with their written consent, label it as AI-generated, and never use it for testimonials or claims about results.
