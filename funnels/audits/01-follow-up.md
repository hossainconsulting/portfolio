# Audit 1: Follow-up audit (enquiry to booked job)

**Purpose:** find where enquiries and quotes stall. This is the core audit and the basis of the free workflow snapshot.
**Inputs:** a 20-minute call, plus one recent enquiry walked through from first message to outcome. Public: the website contact path, GBP phone and message settings.
**Score each 0 = no, 1 = sometimes, 2 = yes, or "not checked".**

## First reply
1. Every channel (phone, text, form, DM, Google messages) lands in one place the owner checks.
2. A person replies within 15 minutes in business hours. (Test: send a real enquiry from a number the client approves, and time it. Only with their agreement.)
3. After hours there is an automatic reply that sets expectations.
4. A missed call triggers a text or voicemail callback within an hour.

## Quote
5. The same basic details are captured every time (address, job type, photos, urgency).
6. Quotes go out within one business day of the visit or call.
7. Quotes are stored where the client can find one by customer name in under a minute.

## Follow-up
8. One named person owns every open quote.
9. Unanswered quotes get a follow-up at about day 2 and day 7.
10. The client can say how many quotes went out last month and how many converted. (Their numbers, marked as theirs.)

## Handover and after
11. The tech receives address, access notes, scope and price in one message.
12. After the job: invoice within 48 hours and a review request.

## Red flags to note
Enquiries in more than three places. Quotes only in someone's head. No-one can say who owns follow-up.

## Quick-win bank (pick three)
- Missed-call text-back on the mobile number.
- Two recurring reminders per open quote (day 2, day 7) and a two-line message template.
- One handover text template.
- A "we reply within X" line on the contact page, only if they can keep it.
- A shared inbox or one list checked twice a day.
