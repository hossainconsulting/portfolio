# Audit 4: Email repurposing audit

**Purpose:** find content in the client's past emails that can be reused on the website, social posts and a follow-up sequence.
**Inputs:** 5 to 10 past emails or newsletters the client chooses to share, with customer names and details removed. Do not ask for customer data.

## Inventory
1. List each email: date, subject, topic, goal, result (only if the client tells you).
2. Identify the useful pieces in each: a tip, a story, a quote, an offer, an FAQ answer.

## Reuse checks
3. At least one email teaches something a customer asks often (reusable as an FAQ or page).
4. At least one tells a short job story (reusable as a post with permission).
5. Subject lines say what is inside.
6. Each email has one clear next step.
7. Links go to live pages and carry campaign tags.

## Sequence and list health
8. A welcome or enquiry-acknowledgement email exists.
9. A quote follow-up email exists (day 2, day 7).
10. Unsubscribe link and sender identity are present (Spam Act).
11. The list was built from people who agreed to receive emails.
12. Sending is regular and sustainable (note frequency).

## Output
A table: email, best piece, where to reuse it (post, page, sequence), effort. Plus three quick wins.

## Quick-win bank
Turn the top email into three posts. Turn a tip into an FAQ on the contact page. Draft a quote follow-up pair from the best email. Add the unsubscribe line if missing.

## Never claim
Open-rate benchmarks as promises, or "deliverability" findings you did not test.
