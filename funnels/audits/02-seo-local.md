# Audit 2: SEO and Google Business Profile audit

**Purpose:** show whether a local customer can find the business and contact it in under a minute.
**Inputs:** website URL, Google Business Profile (GBP) link. Everything is checked from the public side.
**Tools:** the page itself, Google search (an incognito window, signed out), Google Search Console only if the client gives you access, PageSpeed Insights, a mobile phone. Optional first pass: `searchfit-seo:seo-audit` or `brightdata-plugin:seo-audit`. Verify manually.

## Google Business Profile (highest value for trades)
1. Profile exists, is verified, and name, address or service area, and phone match the website.
2. Primary and secondary categories fit the real services.
3. Services, hours and a clear description are filled in, with photos from the last 6 months.
4. Reviews: count, average and whether the owner replies. (Record the number and the date you looked.)

## Website basics
5. The page loads fast on mobile (note the PageSpeed result and date, do not promise a ranking).
6. The phone number is tappable, and a quote or contact button is visible without scrolling.
7. Each main service has its own page naming the service and suburb or area.
8. Title and meta description exist, are not duplicated, and describe the page.

## Trust and technical
9. HTTPS works, no browser warnings, the site works on a phone.
10. Real photos and licence or insurance details are shown. Nothing invented.
11. A form or booking path exists and is tested (submit your own test with the client's agreement).
12. Site appears in a signed-out search for "[service] [suburb]" and for the business name. (Record what you saw and when. This is a snapshot, not a ranking guarantee.)

## Red flags
Wrong phone number. Unclaimed GBP. Site only works as an image. Contact form that goes nowhere.

## Quick-win bank
Fix GBP categories. Add 5 recent photos. Reply to reviews. Make the phone tappable. Write one page for the top service and suburb. Ask each happy customer for a review.

## Never claim
"You will rank first", traffic or revenue forecasts, or a competitor's numbers without a source and date.
