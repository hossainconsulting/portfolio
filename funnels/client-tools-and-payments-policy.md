# Client tools and payments policy (draft)

Decided by Hemayet, 2026-10-14. Keep it simple. This is a working rule, not a contract. Contract wording needs legal review (see "Still open").

## The rule

1. **Clients own every third-party tool and pay for it themselves.** That covers CRM, email platform, ad accounts, domains, hosting, scheduling, AI and video tools, and any other platform.
2. **Clients subscribe with their own card, in their own name,** directly with each provider. Hossain Consulting never pays for a client's tool, never resells one, and never holds a client's card details.
3. **Clients pay Hossain Consulting only its own fees** (setup and management), through a payment provider. No tool costs, no ad spend and no domain or hosting charges pass through Hossain Consulting.
4. **Hossain Consulting works inside the client's accounts** with the least access that does the job, granted by invitation or delegated access.

This matches what the agency site already says on `/services` and `/privacy` (clients own their CRM subscription, account and data, and pay the provider directly).

## What this means in practice

| Item | Who owns and pays | Hossain Consulting does |
|---|---|---|
| CRM, email platform, scheduling | Client | Recommends, shows the provider's price, configures after the client subscribes |
| Paid ads (Google, Meta, others) | Client's ad account, client's card, client's budget | Manages only the agreed work, never spends beyond the written budget, never uses its own card |
| Domain and hosting | Registered and billed in the client's name, client is the owner | Configures DNS and publishing with delegated access |
| AI, avatar, voice and video tools | Client's subscription if they want it | Uses it for the client's work only if the client has subscribed |
| Hossain Consulting's own tools (the audit kit, internal AI tools, its own email list) | Hossain Consulting | Pays them from its own fees. Not charged to clients |
| Hossain Consulting's fees | Client pays Hossain Consulting | Invoices through the payment provider |

## Handling card details and credentials

- Never ask for or accept card numbers by email, chat, DM, form or call. The payment provider's hosted checkout takes the card.
- Never ask for passwords or tokens. Ask for invitations: Google Ads manager access, Meta Business partner access, registrar or Cloudflare delegated users, CRM user invites.
- Remove access when the work ends, as the services page already says.

## Rules that keep this simple

- Before a client subscribes to anything, show them the provider's current price in writing and say it is paid to the provider, not to Hossain Consulting.
- If a provider has a free tier that does the job, say so.
- Disclose any affiliate commission in writing before recommending a tool (already on the services page).
- Ad spend: the client sets the budget in writing. Pause before any change that would exceed it.
- If a client cancels, they cancel their own subscriptions. Hossain Consulting's fee terms are separate.
- A client's price, cooling-off and refund terms apply only to Hossain Consulting's own fees. Nothing is refunded on the client's behalf by Hossain Consulting for tool charges, because Hossain Consulting never took them.

## Why this fixes the earlier conflicts

- No pass-through costs, so no markup disputes, no GST on resold tools, no unrecoverable annual plans, and no chargebacks on tools.
- The cooling-off and refund rules only need to cover Hossain Consulting's own fees, which are easy to refund.
- The agency site's "you own your account and pay the provider directly" wording stays true.
