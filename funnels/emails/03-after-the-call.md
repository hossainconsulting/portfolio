# Sequence 3: After the call (show, no-show, quote)

Trigger: Calendly event ends (show) or Hemayet marks no-show (Calendly "Mark as no-show"). Send by hand or from a template; these are one-to-one replies, not bulk marketing.
No free reports, audits or snapshots are offered. The next step after a call is a written scope and fixed quote, only if the client wants one.

## Same day: thanks (showed up)
Subject: Thanks, {{first_name}}

Thanks for the time today.

What I heard: {{one_line_summary}}.

If you'd like me to scope it, reply "scope it" and I'll send a written scope and a fixed quote including GST, for you to approve first. No pressure either way.
Hemayet

## Day 7 (no reply): check-in
Subject: Want me to scope it?

Hi {{first_name}}, I wanted to check whether you'd like a written scope and fixed quote for what we talked about. If the timing is wrong, no problem. Just let me know.
Hemayet

## Day 21 (no reply): close the loop
Subject: Closing your file

Hi {{first_name}}, I'll stop following up now so I don't clutter your inbox. If things change, reply to this email any time.
Hemayet

## No-show (send within 1 hour)
Subject: Missed you today

Hi {{first_name}}, we missed each other for {{date_time}}. No problem, it happens. Pick a new time here: https://portfolio.hossainconsulting.com/book. If you'd rather skip the call, the checklist is here: https://portfolio.hossainconsulting.com/checklist
Hemayet
(One follow-up only. If no reply after 7 days, stop.)

## After a quote is sent
Day 2: "Any questions on the quote? Happy to adjust the scope." Day 7: "I'll keep the quote open until {{date}}. Let me know either way." Then stop.
