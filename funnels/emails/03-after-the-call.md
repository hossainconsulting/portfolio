# Sequence 3: After the call (show, no-show, proposal)

Trigger: Calendly event ends (show) or Hemayet marks no-show (Calendly "Mark as no-show"). Send by hand or from a template; these are one-to-one replies, not bulk marketing.

## Same day: thanks + what happens next (showed up)

Thanks for the time today. As promised, you'll get a one-page snapshot with up to three quick wins by {{date_plus_3_business_days}}.

What I heard: {{one_line_summary}}.

If a project makes sense after you read it, I'll send a written scope and a fixed quote (+GST) for you to approve first. No pressure either way.
Hemayet

## Day 3: snapshot delivery
Subject: Your workflow snapshot

Attached is your one-page snapshot. It lists where enquiries stall and up to three quick wins, easiest first. The first one needs nothing from me.

Want me to scope the biggest win? Reply "scope it" and I'll send a written scope and fixed quote (+GST) within 3 business days.
Hemayet

## Day 7 (no reply): check-in
Subject: Did the snapshot help?

Hi {{first_name}}, did you get a chance to look at the snapshot? If you tried the first quick win, I'd like to hear what happened. If you'd like the biggest one scoped, say so and I'll send a quote.
Hemayet

## Day 21 (no reply): close the loop
Subject: Closing your file

Hi {{first_name}}, I'll stop following up now so I don't clutter your inbox. The snapshot is yours to keep. If things change, reply to this email any time.
Hemayet

## No-show (send within 1 hour)

Hi {{first_name}}, we missed each other for {{date_time}}. No problem, it happens. Pick a new time here: https://portfolio.hossainconsulting.com/book. If you'd rather I send the checklist and skip the call: https://portfolio.hossainconsulting.com/workflow-snapshot

(One follow-up only. If no reply after 7 days, stop.)
