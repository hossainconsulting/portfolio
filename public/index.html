What this is

These self-directed Salesforce simulations explore requirements, data modelling, security, automation and handover in fictional business settings. They are at different stages of development. Each repository's dated records distinguish completed work, planned scope and unresolved decisions.

Where available, the repositories include configuration, seed data and deliverables such as design documents, SOPs and analyses. Read these alongside dated test results and session notes to understand what was implemented, how it was checked and what remains unfinished.

Disclosure

These are simulations, not client work. SunRise Solar Solutions, Meridian Field Services, TradeLink Group, Meridian Appliance Care, Coastline Retail Group, Ironbark Industrial Supply and Kurrajong Energy are fictional companies. Every scenario, stakeholder and record is invented for the purpose of developing and evidencing implementation skill.

No real customer data appears anywhere in these projects. I'd rather you know that in the first thirty seconds than find out later.
