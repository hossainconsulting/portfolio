Privacy Policy

Plain-language summary: I collect only what you give me, I use it to reply and to send what you asked for, I do not sell it, and you can ask me to delete it.

Who I am

Hossain Consulting, operated by Hemayet Hossain, Sydney NSW (ABN 23 732 235 722). Contact: hemayet@hossainconsulting.com. I handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What I collect

  • Checklist: email signup is paused. The checklist is a direct download and does not require contact details or marketing consent.
  • Bookings: name, email, time, and any notes you type into Calendly.
  • Chat widget and email: whatever you write, and the contact details you choose to give.

Please do not send passwords, payment card numbers or your customers' records.

Why I use it

  • To reply to you and run any call you booked.
  • New checklist email signup is paused. Before signup opens, I will name the email provider and its data locations here, explain the consent choices, and verify delivery and unsubscribe handling. Downloading a resource or booking a call is not marketing consent.
  • To see which pages and campaigns lead to enquiries, so I can improve them.

Who else handles it

I use service providers to run this: Cloudflare (website hosting), Calendly (bookings), Google (email and calendar). No email marketing provider is identified or verified for checklist signup yet. They process data for me and some store it outside Australia, including in the United States. The chat widget sends your messages to Anthropic to generate replies. I do not sell your information or give it to others for their marketing. I may disclose it if the law requires.

Payments

Payment processing is not set up yet. When it is, you will pay my fees through a payment provider that I will name here first. I do not ask for or store your card details. Tools such as a CRM, email platform, ad account, domain or hosting are bought by you, in your own name and with your own card, directly from each provider.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no advertising cookies. Campaign tags from the link are held in your browser tab (session storage) until you close it. If I add analytics or ad pixels later, I will update this page first and ask for consent where required.

Keeping and deleting it

New checklist subscriptions are not accepted while signup is paused. If you previously supplied details, you can contact me to ask what is held, request deletion or withdraw marketing consent. Any necessary record of a request not to receive marketing is kept to respect that request. Lead and call notes are kept for up to 24 months unless we start working together, in which case the engagement terms apply.

Your choices

  • To withdraw any previous marketing consent, email me or reply "unsubscribe" to a message you received.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete what I hold by emailing me. I will reply within 30 days.
  • If you are unhappy with how I handled something, tell me first. You can then complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Security

Access to this information is limited to me and the providers above, behind their own sign-in controls. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to cause you serious harm I will notify you and the OAIC as the law requires.

Other Hossain Consulting sites

The agency site at hossainconsulting.com has its own privacy notice covering enquiries and call bookings there. This policy covers this portfolio site, the checklist and the chat widget.

Changes

I will change the version date above when this policy changes.
