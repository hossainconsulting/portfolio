What this is, and what it is not

Please read this before using the prompts. It is short, and it is here because the subject deserves plain speaking rather than fine print.

1. What Weekend Reset is

Weekend Reset is a small web page that holds ten pre-written prompts about burnout, rest, boundaries and preparing for the working week. You fill in a few blanks and the page assembles the finished prompt, which you can copy or open in an AI assistant of your choosing. That is the whole product. The page does not generate advice itself, does not talk to you, and has no server-side component.

2. Not medical advice, not therapy, not a diagnosis

Nothing on this page, in the prompts, or in the answers an AI assistant gives you is medical, psychological, or psychiatric advice. It is not therapy, counselling, coaching, or treatment, and it does not create a clinician–patient relationship with anyone. The prompts are explicitly written to ask the assistant not to act as a therapist and not to diagnose you, but an AI assistant can ignore an instruction, misunderstand you, or be confidently wrong.

Burnout, persistent exhaustion, low mood, anxiety, and sleep problems can have medical causes and can be signs of conditions that need proper care. If your symptoms are severe, have lasted more than a couple of weeks, or are getting in the way of daily life, please see a general practitioner, psychologist, or other qualified professional. Do not delay or stop professional care because of anything you read here or receive from an AI assistant.

3. Not for emergencies

If you are in immediate danger, or you are thinking about harming yourself or someone else, do not use this page. Contact a person now.

4. The AI assistant is a third party

When you press “Open in Claude” or “Open in ChatGPT”, your browser opens a new tab at that provider with your finished prompt in the address. From that moment the text, and everything you type afterwards, is handled by that provider under their terms of service and privacy policy, not this site’s. If you would rather not send the text that way, use “Copy prompt” and paste it wherever you like. This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Anthropic, OpenAI, or any other AI provider; their product names are used only to describe where a link goes.

AI assistants generate text by prediction. They can produce plausible-sounding suggestions that are inaccurate, unsafe, or unsuitable for you. Use your own judgement, and treat anything that sounds like a medical or legal instruction with particular caution.

5. Your data

  • This page runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to this site’s operator, and there is no account, sign-in, or database.
  • Your drafts, your “done” ticks, and your acknowledgement of this disclaimer are stored in your browser’s local storage on this device only. Anyone with access to this browser profile could read them. “Erase everything stored here” in the page footer removes them.
  • The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. Cloudflare, which serves the page, may keep standard server logs such as IP address and user agent under its own policies.
  • The “Open in” links place your prompt text in a URL, which that provider (and your browser history) will record.

6. Who this is for

Weekend Reset is written for adults who are tired and want a structured way to think about it. It is not designed for children, and it is not a tool for employers, schools, or clinicians to assess anyone. Nothing here should be used to make decisions about another person’s health or work.
