How to use Claude with Microsoft 365

Most people use Microsoft 365 every day. Almost none of them use Claude with it. Here are seven places it earns its keep.

The workflow: steps across Microsoft 365

01

Outlook

Manage emails, save time, and communicate with clarity.

  • Summarise long email threads in seconds
  • Extract key points, action items and deadlines
  • Draft, rewrite or improve email responses
  • Adjust tone: professional, friendly, concise
Example Summarise a 15-email thread, pull out the action items, and draft a concise reply that moves the conversation forward.
02

Word

Create, edit and refine documents faster.

  • Draft first versions from an outline or prompt
  • Rewrite and strengthen content for clarity
  • Improve structure, flow and tone
  • Shorten, expand or simplify sections
Example Turn rough notes into a polished document, improve the clarity, and get it ready to share.
03

Excel

Work with data, find insights, and make better decisions.

  • Explain what your data means in plain language
  • Identify trends, patterns and outliers
  • Help with formulas, functions and calculations
  • Create summaries, charts and recommendations
Example Explain last quarter’s sales data, spot the trends, and suggest next steps to improve performance.
04

PowerPoint

Turn ideas into engaging presentations.

  • Turn content or documents into slide outlines
  • Create compelling titles, headings and copy
  • Suggest visuals, layouts and slide flow
  • Generate speaker notes and key takeaways
Example Create a 10-slide deck outline, write the key messages, and generate the speaker notes for your presentation.
05

OneNote

Capture ideas, organise notes, and build knowledge.

  • Turn messy notes into clear, organised summaries
  • Extract action items, decisions and next steps
  • Create checklists, templates and frameworks
  • Connect ideas and build knowledge bases
Example Convert meeting notes into action items, assign owners, and organise them by project.
06

Teams

Collaborate effectively and stay aligned.

  • Summarise meeting transcripts and chats
  • Highlight decisions, risks and action items
  • Draft follow-up messages and announcements
  • Create agendas and status updates
Example Summarise a Teams meeting, list the key decisions and action items, and draft a follow-up message for the team.
07

OneDrive

Find files, organise content, and surface insights.

  • Find the right files and information fast
  • Summarise documents across multiple files
  • Compare versions and pull key takeaways
  • Organise and tag content for easy access
Example Find the relevant project files, summarise the key documents, and create a one-page executive summary.

How the connection actually works

None of the above requires Microsoft to ship anything. Some of it runs through Claude’s connectors, which read your mail, calendar and files directly; the rest is paste-in-and-ask, which costs nothing to try and works from any plan.

Which connectors are available to you depends on your Claude plan and on what your Microsoft 365 administrator permits, so check the connector list in your own account before designing a workflow around one.

The part worth keeping

The pattern under all seven steps is the same: Claude is good at the reading, the drafting and the summarising, and you stay responsible for the judgement. Ask it to explain the data, not to decide what to do about it. Ask it to draft the reply, not to send it.

That division is also what makes the output safe to put in front of a client — which is the standard the rest of this site is built to.
