# Mindset: a 12-week personal development and emotional intelligence programme

The journal (`tradelab journal`) is the measuring instrument. Every exercise
produces a number in it. Money is never the goal of a week; process is.

## The five emotional scales (logged before every trade, 1 = none, 5 = extreme)

| scale | what it feels like | what it does to trades |
|---|---|---|
| calm | steady breathing, curiosity about the outcome | the only state to trade from |
| fatigue | heavy eyes, skipped steps | missed stops, sloppy sizing |
| urgency | "I have to get in now" | chasing, bad entries |
| revenge | "I'll get it back" | size-ups after losses, the martingale |
| fomo | "everyone else is making money" | buying tops, abandoning the plan |

`tradelab journal check` flags three losses in a row, size-ups after a loss, hot
states at entry, off-plan trades and overtrading. A flag means stop, not think.

## The programme

**Weeks 1-2: awareness.** No trades. Read *Trading in the Zone* (Douglas).
Twice a day, log the five scales without trading. Learn what your baseline is.
Output: 28 state entries, a one-page "when am I not calm" note.

**Weeks 3-4: the plan.** Write the plan: one setup, entry rule, stop rule, exit
rule, size rule (1% risk), daily halt (-3%), weekly halt (-6%). Read the
pre-trade checklist (`tradelab journal checklist`) aloud before each paper
trade. Output: the plan in `docs/PLAN-TRADING.md`, 10 paper trades with
checklist ticked.

**Weeks 5-6: process goals.** Goal is 100% plan adherence, not profit. Every
off-plan trade gets a written paragraph: what state, what trigger, what to
change. Output: adherence number from `tradelab journal review`.

**Weeks 7-8: losing well.** Steenbarger's *Daily Trading Coach*, lessons on
drawdowns. Practise the recovery protocol: after a halt, close the platform,
walk 20 minutes, write three sentences, do not return that day. Output: a
recovery protocol that was actually used at least once.

**Weeks 9-10: the review habit.** Sunday: `tradelab journal review`, compare
pnl-when-hot vs pnl-when-cool, and one change to the plan at most. Output: two
weekly reviews with the change and its reason.

**Weeks 11-12: identity.** The goal shifts from "make money" to "be a person who
executes a positive-expectancy process without exception". Write the letter to
yourself for the next drawdown. Output: expectancy in R and adherence over the
12 weeks, on one page, dated.
